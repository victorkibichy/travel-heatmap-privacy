Privacy Policy

My Travel Heatmap – Last updated: October 8, 2026

My Travel Heatmap ("the app") builds a heat map of the places you have been. Your travel data is personal, so the app is designed to keep it on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses and how it is used.

Summary

  • We do not run a server that collects your data.
  • We do not track you, show ads, or use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Information the app uses

  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your device location (including low-power visit detection in the background) to notice places where you stay and to show your position on the globe. Places are stored as hexagonal zones rather than exact coordinates.
  • Photos. With your permission, the app reads the date and location metadata of your geotagged photos to add places to your map, and shows photos you took at a place when you open it. Photos are not copied, modified, or uploaded; they remain in your Photos library.
  • Place names. To label places, the app may ask Apple's mapping services to convert a zone's location into a place name. This request is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Where your data is stored

Your visits, places, and statistics are stored locally on your device. If iCloud sync is offered and you enable it, your data is stored in your own private iCloud account (CloudKit private database), which only you can access and which is governed by Apple's terms. We, the developer, cannot see it.

Sharing

Social features such as friends and leaderboards share only what you choose to share, and only with the people you choose. You can control or turn off sharing in the app's Settings. We do not share your data for advertising or any other purpose.

Tracking and third parties

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries.

Your choices and control

  • You can change or revoke Location and Photos permissions at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • You can delete your data by deleting the app, which removes locally stored data. If you enabled iCloud sync, you can also remove the app's data in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
